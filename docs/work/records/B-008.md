**Next action / closure evidence:** 2026-11-25: cause found, a test-rig race, not feed logic. The e2e rig ran every pump at 1000 times its flow. The simulated pumps deliver for as long as they are really on, and on a busy runner each pump goes off tens of ms late. A feed's water is five 20 ms runs, so about 50 ms of lateness per run added up to 4.7 L to a 2 L batch. The transfer's allowance (1.5 times the batch, one 120 ms slice) could not move that, so the feed ended `done` with the tank full. On the real rig a run lasts seconds and the same lateness is a few mL. Fix in [#38](https://github.com/voidbrain/Grover/pull/38): `apps/hub/test/fast-rig.ts` runs supply pumps at 100 times their flow and transfer pumps at 1000 times. No assertion or timeout changed. 60 of 60 runs passed under the same load. Next: Verified after the next 10 CI runs pass this test.
