## Comparison

| Concept | Disposition | Level | Why | Why not | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Write-then-forward outbox on the device, acknowledgment-gated, with idempotent inserts upstream | adopt (keep) | project | The common edge design: the local store is the source of truth and the link is best effort; matches the agent's outbox and the hub's unique keys | — | [IIoT reference architecture, edge](https://gauravs19.github.io/iiot-reference-architecture/hardware/edge); [silently lost MQTT messages](https://dev.to/ranaweerasupun/why-your-mqtt-client-is-silently-losing-messages-and-how-i-fixed-it-robmqtt-4n4k) |
| Device re-checks every safety limit itself; hub plans, device executes or aborts | adopt (keep) | project | Outside edge guidance keeps control local during outages; a pump or heater must not depend on the hub | — | [IoT For All: beyond the broker](https://www.iotforall.com/beyond-the-mqtt-broker-iot-data-architecture) |
| Monotonic per-agent sequence numbers on outbox events | watch | project | Outside practice pairs the outbox with a sequence for ordering and gap detection, beyond deduplication | Idempotent unique keys already make replay safe; ordering is by reading time. Reconsider if a replay out of order ever shows a wrong state | [silently lost MQTT messages](https://dev.to/ranaweerasupun/why-your-mqtt-client-is-silently-losing-messages-and-how-i-fixed-it-robmqtt-4n4k) |
